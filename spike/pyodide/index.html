Bytes are what the browser reports for this page load. Cross-origin responses only report a size when the server sends Timing-Allow-Origin; anything that does not shows as not disclosed. A reload runs against a warm HTTP cache, which is the number a returning angler sees — the cold one above is the first visit.

What a pass means. The browser loaded CPython and shapely, built the 5 m grid from the real lake outline, ray-cast the effective fetch for one wind direction, scored every cell through the YAML expression, and matched the server's answer. If that holds, the twelve pre-baked wind buckets in the Pages build could become one live computation at any bearing.

What it does not mean. Nothing about whether to adopt it. That turns on the cold-load seconds above, measured on a phone on the bank — not on a desktop with a warm cache.
