{#- Ambient fish. Six species crossing the page from time to time, per the owner's brief, and made more frequent at their request (2026-08-28): shorter cycles and a tighter stagger, so a crossing happens every few seconds rather than every half minute. Decorative only: the layer is aria-hidden and pointer-events:none, it states nothing the page does not already say in words, and it can never swallow a tap meant for a control underneath it. Zero bytes on the wire - inline SVG and CSS keyframes, no sprite, no library. Motion is transform/opacity only, pauses when the tab is hidden, and is removed entirely under prefers-reduced-motion (style.css, "Ambient fish and tackle"). Every species owns its geometry, per CLAUDE.md, and the TAIL ALONE has to identify it: roach - even, moderate fork bream - long unequal fork, lower lobe dropped well past the upper crucian - rounded convex fan, no fork at all rudd - even fork on a deeper body, snout turned up carp - broad SHALLOW fork, half the notch depth and twice the width ide - deep even fork on a long torpedo body Recolouring one silhouette six times would teach the angler nothing and is explicitly forbidden. THE DORSAL IS PART OF THE BODY OUTLINE, not a stroke arc floating above it. The first version drew it as a separate arc and it rendered as a detached eyebrow on every fish - obvious the moment the six were put in a row at size, invisible in the markup. The crucian's fan tail had the same fault and read as a ball trailing the fish. Colours are the softs and the water tones only - never a day-band MARK, and no longer the band tints either: on kraft the pale tints were barely visible, and keeping decoration off the band hues entirely is the safer reading of the reserved rule (docs/20 §1). The fill lives in `style="fill: ..."`, not in a fill="" attribute: var() does not resolve in an SVG presentation attribute in Chrome. -#}
{#- roach: slender, modest dorsal hump, even moderate fork -#} {#- bream: deep slab body, small head, lower tail lobe dropped well past the upper - the most distinctive tail of the six -#} {#- crucian: near-circular body, long low dorsal, and a rounded convex fan tail attached to the body - no fork anywhere on this fish -#} {#- rudd: deeper than the roach, dorsal set well back, snout turned up, even fork -#} {#- carp: thick body, a LONG low dorsal ridge rather than a hump, and a broad shallow fork - the notch is half the depth of the roach's and twice the width. No barbels: CLAUDE.md says outright that barbels are garnish and the silhouette carries the recognition, and at this size they crossed the body outline and read as a drawing error. -#} {#- ide: long torpedo body, small dorsal, and the deepest even fork of the six - the same family as the roach, cut twice as deep -#}
