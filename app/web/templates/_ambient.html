{#- Ambient fish. Four species crossing the page from time to time, per the owner's brief. Decorative only: the layer is aria-hidden and pointer-events:none, it states nothing the page does not already say in words, and it can never swallow a tap meant for a control underneath it. Zero bytes on the wire - inline SVG and CSS keyframes, no sprite, no library. Motion is transform/opacity only, pauses when the tab is hidden, and is removed entirely under prefers-reduced-motion (style.css, "Ambient fish and tackle"). Every species owns its geometry, per CLAUDE.md. The tail alone identifies each one: roach an even fork, bream a long unequal fork with the lower lobe dropped, crucian a rounded convex fan, rudd an even fork on a deeper body with an upturned mouth. Recolouring one silhouette four times would teach the angler nothing and is explicitly forbidden. --cross is the full cycle; --delay staggers them so they are occasional rather than a parade. --top is where the arc happens. -#}
