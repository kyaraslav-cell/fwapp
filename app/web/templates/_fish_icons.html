{# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Per-species fish icons, hand-drawn in this repo. Not taken from an icon library. PhyloPic and SciDraw both offer CC0 species silhouettes, but they mean a network fetch or a vendored asset folder plus licence bookkeeping, in an app meant to run offline on a bank with no build step. These are a few KB of inline SVG with no attribution burden. THE THING THAT KEPT GOING WRONG. Two earlier rounds "redrew" these and the grid still read as one fish repeated. Two separate causes, both now fixed: 1. Nothing was rendering them. `shape` arrived on the Species model after the table had been seeded, the seed refused to backfill, so every species resolved to `fish_icon(None)` -> `#fish-roach`. See `app/notebook/species.py`. Six roach, whatever was drawn here. 2. The drawings really were near-identical. Roach, bream, rudd, ide, carp and crucian all shared ONE rounded-oval body and differed only in colour and tail. On a 46px button that is the same fish six times. The fix for (2) is BODY DEPTH, because depth is the only thing that still reads at 20 device pixels. Detail does not survive that size; outline does. Measured as body depth against the 100-unit canvas: ide 22 clean torpedo, small head, brightest silver roach 27 moderate, blunt head, red fins carp 34 long and heavy, big head, 4 barbels, LONG arched dorsal rudd 37 deep, UPTURNED mouth, dorsal set well back, vivid red fins bream 43 slab-sided rhomboid, tiny head, very long anal fin crucian 45 near-circular disc, convex dorsal, NO barbels, ROUND tail So the six quick-log species run from a 22-unit torpedo to a 45-unit disc, and no two are within 3 units of each other. `tests/test_species_seed.py` asserts they never collapse onto a shared shape again. The secondary silhouettes keep their original 0 0 100 44 viewBox and are centred automatically inside the taller canvas by `preserveAspectRatio`: tench thick and blunt, every fin ROUNDED, paddle tail, red eye pike long and low, DUCK-BILL snout, dorsal and anal far back perch steep hump behind head, SPINY first dorsal, dark bars zander long perch: slimmer, two dorsals, pale catfish broad flat head, LONG barbels, very long anal, rounded tail eel ribbon body, continuous fin fringe, no fork trout torpedo with ADIPOSE fin and spots small slender silver minnow for bleak, gudgeon, dace, loach Head faces LEFT in every icon. The dive animation depends on this - the CSS rotation direction assumes a left-facing head (see style.css dive keyframes). --------------------------------------------------------------------------- #} {# display:none on the sprite root stops Chrome resolving the gradient references from elsewhere on the page - the bodies render empty.
