## The feeling curve

Written before the acts, per feel.md §1.

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Seen** | The instrument boots with the field blank. It does not know either. That blank is the visitor's own morning on the bank. |
| 2 | **Alert** | Real measurements arrive and populate the surface. Pressure, wind, water. The thing is awake and it is reading an actual lake. |
| 3 | **Sceptical** | The page says the objection out loud, in its own status line, before the visitor can. Quiet act. Deliberately the least happening on the page. |
| 4 | **Arrested** | The prediction composes, hashes itself from the numbers on screen, and seals. **PEAK.** |
| 5 | **Sober** | The register's real rows. 23 anglers, 0.71 kg a day, blanks included. Nobody's marketing number. |
| 6 | **Resolved** | A cursor in a real field. The visitor does the thing rather than being asked to. |

No two adjacent feelings repeat. Act 3 is the quiet one immediately before the
peak, which is what gives act 4 its room.

## The peak

**Act 4, The Seal.** Largest span on the page by a clear margin: 4.2 against a
next-largest of 2.6.

The sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> "It writes down its guess before you go, and then you literally cannot scroll
> back and change it."

## Authored silence

Act 3 carries deliberate quiet: one line, a wipe, and no populating panels. The
verification pass must not read it as dead scroll. It exists so the seal has
something to land against.

## The tell-someone sentence

> It's the site where **the app commits to a guess in front of you and can never
> take it back.**
